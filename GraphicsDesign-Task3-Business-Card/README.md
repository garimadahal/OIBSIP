## 📝 Design Rationale

The PixelCraft business card is designed with a clean, modern, and professional layout that reflects the brand's creative identity. A purple and blue color palette represents creativity, innovation, and trust, while the use of whitespace ensures excellent readability. The front highlights the company's branding, and the back organizes essential contact information in a simple and visually appealing way for both digital and print use.
