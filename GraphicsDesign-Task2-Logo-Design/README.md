# 🎨 Task 2 – Logo Design

## 📌 Objective

Design a professional and minimalist logo for a fictional brand that communicates the brand's identity through simple shapes, typography, and color.

---

## 🏢 Brand Name

**PixelCraft**

**Tagline:** *Design. Create. Inspire.*

---

## 📖 Project Description

This project presents a complete logo identity for **PixelCraft**, a fictional creative design agency specializing in graphic design, UI/UX design, branding, and digital solutions.

The logo combines a modern pixel element with a stylized letter **P**, representing creativity, innovation, technology, and digital craftsmanship. The branding follows a clean and minimalist approach while maintaining versatility for both digital and print applications.

---

## ✨ Features

- Original minimalist logo design
- Full Color Logo
- Black Monochrome Version
- White Reversed Version
- Icon-only Logo Variations
- Carefully selected color palette

- Print-ready design

## 💡 Design Rationale

The PixelCraft logo represents creativity, innovation, and precision in digital design. The pixel-inspired elements symbolize technology and digital transformation, while the stylized "P" creates a unique and memorable brand identity. The purple and blue color palette conveys creativity, trust, professionalism, and modern digital innovation.
